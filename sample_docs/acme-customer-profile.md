# Customer Profile — Acme Corp

## Account overview

Acme Corp is a mid-market manufacturing and distribution company headquartered in Bengaluru with ~4,200 employees. Annual contract value with us is USD 2.3M across the Digital Platform programme and a managed analytics service. Relationship began in 2023 with a data warehouse modernisation project delivered on time; customer satisfaction score last quarter was 8.4/10.

## Key stakeholders

- **Priya Sharma — CIO.** Executive sponsor. Decisive, values direct communication and dislikes long slide decks; prefers a one-page summary with clear asks. Champions the platform internally. Cares most about the September go-live date and user adoption.
- **Sanjay Rao — CFO.** Commercial approver. Cost-sensitive; challenged CR pricing twice in Phase 1. Strong stance on data residency: all production data must stay in the Mumbai region (ap-south-1). Not usually in steering committees but reads the minutes.
- **Daniel Weber — PMO Lead.** Day-to-day counterpart. Thorough but stretched across three programmes; actions assigned to him often need follow-up. Good ally for unblocking internal Acme dependencies.
- **Anita Krishnan — Head of InfoSec.** Gatekeeper for the SSO and any integration touching identity or PII. Known for detailed reviews that take two to three weeks; engage early.

## Commercial notes

- Master services agreement runs to March 2027; renewal conversation should start January 2027.
- Acme procurement requires purchase-order coverage before any CR work starts — no letter-of-intent exceptions.
- Phase 3 (supplier portal) is a qualified opportunity, estimated USD 900K, contingent on a successful September go-live.

## History and sensitivities

- A Phase 1 payment milestone was disputed in Nov 2024 over ambiguous acceptance criteria; since then all milestones carry explicit acceptance checklists.
- Acme's previous vendor was replaced after repeated go-live delays — schedule credibility is the single most important trust factor for this account.
